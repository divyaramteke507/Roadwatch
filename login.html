WHY BIOMETRIC VERIFICATION?

Facial recognition ensures that only you can access your secure government profile. This additional layer protects against identity theft and unauthorized access to sensitive national services.

SECURITY DIRECTIVE Empowering Citizens through Protected Digital Identity

Military-Grade Encryption

Your connection to the National Citizen Database is protected by AES-256 end-to-end encryption. All transmitted data is secured against unauthorized interception.

Multi-Factor Authentication

Access requires mandatory verification via registered mobile OTP and dynamic AI captcha verification, ensuring only the true citizen can securely log in.

Zero Data Retention Policy

In accordance with the Data Protection Act, your session and biometric authentication tokens are instantly purged from the servers immediately after identity verification.
