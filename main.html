Frequently Asked Questions
What is RoadWatch National Portal?

RoadWatch is an AI-powered portal allowing citizens to report road and infrastructure issues directly to local authorities using geotagged photos.

How does Gemini Vision AI help?

The integrated AI automatically analyzes uploaded photos to determine the severity of damage and categorizes the issue (e.g., critical pothole) to prioritize repairs.

Is my personal data secure?

Yes. Your login is protected by advanced biometric verification and OTP. Session data is encrypted and handled only over secure government networks.

Can I track my complaints?

Currently, you can submit reports directly. In future updates, a tracking dashboard will be made available to see the resolution status of reported issues.

What should I do if the AI categorizes my issue wrongly?

You can manually change the category and severity from the dropdown lists provided on the report form before submission.
